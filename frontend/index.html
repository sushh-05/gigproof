GigProof

Check your social security eligibility across every app you drive or deliver for.

The Rule

Under India's Social Security (Central) Rules, 2026, gig workers qualify for benefits with 90 qualifying days with one aggregator, or 120 days combined across multiple apps, in the current financial year. No single app shows you this combined number — GigProof does.
